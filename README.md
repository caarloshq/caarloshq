**Human design and decisions, built for products and ready for AI.**

I'm a Product Designer based in Brazil, working across product strategy, design systems, and AI-ready workflows.

I started in branding and moved into digital products. Today, I connect what a brand promises with what the interface delivers, then carry that decision into flows, components, documentation, and working software.

I have been working with design for seven years. During this period, I have worked on projects for brands such as **QuintoAndar**, **Magazine Luiza**, and **SPC Brasil**.
